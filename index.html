    Frequently asked questions

    What documents are required for income tax return filing?

    Usually your PAN and Aadhaar, Form 16 if you are salaried, your Annual Information Statement (AIS) and Form 26AS, bank statements and interest certificates, capital gains statements, and proof of any deductions you claim. Businesses also need their books of account or profit and loss figures. We send you a checklist for your case before we start.

    How can a business register for GST?

    Registration is generally required once aggregate turnover crosses Rs 40 lakh for goods or Rs 20 lakh for services in Tamil Nadu, and in some cases, such as most inter-State supplies of goods, regardless of turnover. The application is made on the GST portal with PAN, Aadhaar authentication, proof of the place of business and bank details. We check whether registration applies to you and file the application.

    What accounting services do you provide?

    Bookkeeping on Tally and cloud accounting software, bank, customer and supplier reconciliations, monthly and quarterly MIS reports, year-end finalisation and support to your auditors, and payroll with professional tax compliance.

    I have received a tax notice. What should I do?

    Do not ignore it, and note the date by which a reply is due. Send us the notice on WhatsApp and we will tell you what it needs and by when. Every notice reply we prepare is reviewed personally by our principal before it is filed.

    Can startups outsource bookkeeping and compliance?

    Yes. We can register the business, then maintain the books and handle GST, TDS, ROC and PF and ESI filings on an agreed schedule, so the founders can focus on the business.
