## Photography and map data

Wealth Bridge Tax & Business Consultants is an independent tax and business consulting firm, owned and operated as a proprietorship by S. Dineshbabu.

The firm provides tax, business advisory and compliance services. Attest functions and other services that the law reserves for specified licensed or regulated professionals are not undertaken by the firm. Where an engagement requires such a service, it is performed by an independent professional licensed for that purpose.

Representation before tax and regulatory authorities is undertaken only in the capacity permitted to an authorised representative under the applicable law. Trademark and patent filings are made through registered agents. Legal vetting of documents, where required, is carried out in coordination with advocates.

The firm does not guarantee the outcome of any proceeding, application or subsidy claim. Outcomes depend on the facts, the law and the decision of the authority, bank or agency concerned.
