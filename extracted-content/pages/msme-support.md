## Frequently asked questions

### Q: Is Udyam registration free?
Yes. Registration on the Udyam portal carries no government fee. Our fee covers classification, filing and linking it correctly with your GST and PAN.

### Q: What is the 45-day payment rule?
Under the Income-tax Act, payments due to registered micro and small suppliers that are made beyond the time allowed under the MSME Act are not allowed as a deduction until actually paid. Buyers therefore have a strong reason to pay MSMEs on time.

### Q: What can I do if a buyer delays payment?
A registered micro or small enterprise can file a delayed payment application through the MSME Samadhaan portal for the Facilitation Council's consideration. We prepare the application and supporting documents.

### Q: Can MSMEs get loans without collateral?
Eligible loans to micro and small enterprises can be covered under the CGTMSE credit guarantee, which allows banks to lend without collateral security.
