## Documents you will need

- Aadhaar, PAN and educational certificates of the promoter
- Community and other certificates, where the scheme requires
- Quotations for machinery and equipment
- Details of land or building (owned or leased)
- Udyam registration, where available
Scheme availability, eligibility and benefit amounts are decided by the government and change from time to time. Sanction and release are at the discretion of the implementing agency and the lending bank.

## Frequently asked questions

### Q: Which subsidy schemes am I eligible for?
It depends on your age, education, category, location, sector and project cost. Many schemes are credit-linked and require a bank loan. We assess your profile against current guidelines before recommending a scheme.

### Q: Do I need a bank loan to get a subsidy?
Most MSME subsidy schemes are linked to a bank loan, and the subsidy is released through the bank. Some state incentives are claimed after the unit starts production.

### Q: Can an existing business apply for a subsidy?
Some schemes support expansion, diversification or technology upgradation of existing units. Others are only for new enterprises. We check this for you.

### Q: How long does sanction take?
Timelines depend on the scheme, the implementing agency and the bank. A complete application and a sound project report reduce avoidable delays.
