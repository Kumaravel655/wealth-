## Frequently asked questions

### Q: When is PF registration mandatory?
Establishments with 20 or more employees in covered classes must register under the EPF Act. Smaller establishments can register voluntarily.

### Q: When does ESI apply?
ESI generally applies to establishments with 10 or more employees in notified areas, for employees earning up to ₹21,000 a month.

### Q: What is the due date for PF and ESI payments?
Both PF and ESI contributions for a month are due by the 15th of the following month.

### Q: What is a Section 7A inquiry?
It is a proceeding where the PF authority determines the amount of dues payable by an employer. The employer is given an opportunity to produce records and be heard before the order is passed.
