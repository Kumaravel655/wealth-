About Wealth Bridge

An independent tax and business consulting firm, founded in Vellore in 2007 and now serving clients from Vellore and Chennai.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ OUR STORY & HISTORY ]
Wealth Bridge began in Vellore in 2007 with a simple purpose: to give business owners and families the careful, dependable tax advice that is usually available only to large companies.

  * 2007: The practice opens in Vellore and grows from return filing into a full consulting firm.
  * 2017: When GST arrives, we build a GST practice that today handles monthly compliance for many businesses and a steady stream of scrutiny, show cause and appeal proceedings. Our income tax practice grows alongside it, with a strong focus on assessments, reassessment and appeals.
  * Growth: Clients also come to us for bank finance and government support. That work becomes a practice of its own: central and Tamil Nadu subsidy schemes, detailed project reports and end-to-end MSME support.
  * Today: With the opening of our Chennai office in Vadapalani, we serve clients from both cities.

[ WHAT WE STAND FOR - PRACTICE VALUES ]
  * Integrity: Honest advice, even when it is not the easy answer.
  * Diligence: We read the law, check the numbers and verify before we file.
  * Confidentiality: What clients share with us stays with us.
  * Accountability: We own our work and our deadlines.

[ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS & ANSWERS ]

Q1: What is the due date for filing an income tax return?
A1: For individuals and others whose accounts do not need an audit, the due date is generally 31 July after the end of the year. Later dates apply to cases that require an audit, and the government sometimes extends dates. We track the applicable date for each client.

Q2: Which law applies to my return now?
A2: Income earned up to 31 March 2026 is governed by the Income-tax Act, 1961. From 1 April 2026 the Income-tax Act, 2025 applies, and the "tax year" replaces the earlier "previous year" and "assessment year".

Q3: Should I choose the old regime or the new regime?
A3: The new regime is the default and suits most people who do not claim large deductions. If you claim significant deductions such as housing loan interest or investments, the old regime may be better. We compare both before filing.

Q4: I missed the due date. Can I still file?
A4: Yes. A belated return can be filed within the time allowed, and an updated return (ITR-U) can be filed later on payment of additional tax. Late fees and interest may apply.

Q5: My AIS shows income I did not receive. What should I do?
A5: You can submit feedback on the AIS through the income tax portal. We review the AIS with you before filing and raise feedback where information is wrong.

[ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS & ANSWERS ]

Q1: What are the due dates for GST returns?
A1: For monthly filers, GSTR-1 is due by the 11th and GSTR-3B by the 20th of the following month. Quarterly filers under QRMP in Tamil Nadu file GSTR-3B by the 22nd of the month after the quarter. Dates can be extended by notification.

A2: Late fees and interest apply. If returns are pending for a continuous period, e-way bill generation can be blocked and the department can start cancellation of registration.

Q3: What is the QRMP scheme?
A3: Taxpayers with aggregate turnover up to ₹5 crore can file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B quarterly while paying tax monthly. It reduces the number of returns but not the payment discipline.

Q4: Do I need to file GSTR-9?
A4: GSTR-9 is the annual return. Small taxpayers below the notified turnover limit have been exempted in recent years, and GSTR-9C applies to larger taxpayers. We confirm your requirement each year.

Q5: Why is my input tax credit lower than my purchase invoices?
A5: Credit is available only when the supplier reports the invoice in their GSTR-1 and it appears in your GSTR-2B, subject to other conditions. Our monthly reconciliation identifies such gaps early so you can follow up with suppliers.

[ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS & ANSWERS ]

Q1: How long do I have to reply to an income tax notice?
A1: The reply date is stated in the notice and is often short. If more time is needed, an adjournment can be requested through the portal with reasons. Do not let the date pass without a reply or a request.

Q2: What is the time limit to file an appeal before the Commissioner (Appeals)?
A2: Generally 30 days from receipt of the order. The Tribunal appeal is generally due within 60 days of the appellate order. Delay can be condoned in some cases with sufficient cause.

Q3: Do I have to pay the demand while my appeal is pending?
A3: You can apply for stay of demand. The department usually expects a part-payment of the disputed demand while the appeal is pending, depending on the facts.

Q4: Are hearings in person?
A4: Most assessments and first appeals are now faceless and handled online. Personal hearing by video conference can be requested where needed.

Q5: Which Act applies to my notice?
A5: Proceedings for years up to 2025-26 continue under the Income-tax Act, 1961. The Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from Tax Year 2026-27 onwards.

[ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS & ANSWERS ]

Q1: What is the time limit for a GST first appeal?
A1: An appeal to the Appellate Authority must be filed within three months of the date of communication of the order. The authority can condone a further delay of up to one month for sufficient cause.

Q2: Is a pre-deposit required to file a GST appeal?
A2: Yes. The admitted amount must be paid in full, along with a pre-deposit of 10% of the disputed tax, subject to a statutory ceiling. A further pre-deposit applies for appeals to the Tribunal.

Q3: I agree with the discrepancy in an ASMT-10 notice. What should I do?
A3: You can pay the tax with interest through Form DRC-03 and inform the officer in your reply. This usually closes the scrutiny without further proceedings.

Q4: Can the penalty in a show cause notice be reduced?
A4: In non-fraud cases, paying the tax and interest within the time allowed after the notice can significantly reduce or remove the penalty. The options depend on the section and period involved.

Q5: Can you help if the GST department issues summons?
A5: Yes. We help you understand the scope of the enquiry, prepare the documents and statements required, and coordinate with counsel where the matter needs it.

[ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS & ANSWERS ]

Q1: When is PF registration mandatory?
A1: Establishments with 20 or more employees in covered classes must register under the EPF Act. Smaller establishments can register voluntarily.

Q2: When does ESI apply?
A2: ESI generally applies to establishments with 10 or more employees in notified areas, for employees earning up to ₹21,000 a month.

Q3: What is the due date for PF and ESI payments?
A3: Both PF and ESI contributions for a month are due by the 15th of the following month.

Q4: What is a Section 7A inquiry?
A4: It is a proceeding where the PF authority determines the amount of dues payable by an employer. The employer is given an opportunity to produce records and be heard before the order is passed.

[ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS & ANSWERS ]

Q1: Is Udyam registration free?
A1: Yes. Registration on the Udyam portal carries no government fee. Our fee covers classification, filing and linking it correctly with your GST and PAN.

Q2: What is the 45-day payment rule?
A2: Under the Income-tax Act, payments due to registered micro and small suppliers that are made beyond the time allowed under the MSME Act are not allowed as a deduction until actually paid. Buyers therefore have a strong reason to pay MSMEs on time.

Q3: What can I do if a buyer delays payment?
A3: A registered micro or small enterprise can file a delayed payment application through the MSME Samadhaan portal for the Facilitation Council's consideration. We prepare the application and supporting documents.

Q4: Can MSMEs get loans without collateral?
A4: Eligible loans to micro and small enterprises can be covered under the CGTMSE credit guarantee, which allows banks to lend without collateral security.

Answers to common questions about income tax, GST, notices, subsidies and working with us.

[ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS & ANSWERS ]

Q1: What does Wealth Bridge do?
A1: Wealth Bridge Tax & Business Consultants is a tax and business consulting firm in Vellore and Chennai. Our main work is income tax and GST return filing, replies to notices, hearings and appeals under income tax, GST, the Companies Act and PF & ESI laws, central and Tamil Nadu government subsidies, detailed project reports and MSME support.

Q2: Where are your offices?
A2: Our head office is at Union Bank Upstairs, No. 23, 8th East Main Road, Gandhinagar, Katpadi, Vellore 632006. Our Chennai office is at No. 23/1, 1st Floor, Alagiri Nagar 5th Street, 100 Feet Road, Vadapalani, Chennai 600026.

Q3: Do you work with clients outside Vellore and Chennai?
A3: Yes. Most tax filings and proceedings are now online, and we serve clients across Tamil Nadu and India.

Q4: Who handles my work?
A4: Every engagement has a dedicated team member, and significant filings and all notices, hearings and appeals are reviewed personally by our Principal Advisor.

Q5: How are your fees decided?
A5: Fees depend on the scope and complexity of the work. We agree the scope and fee with you before starting.

Q6: How do I share documents with you?
A6: You can share them on WhatsApp, by email, or in person at either office. Documents are accessed only by the team members working on your matter.

Q7: Can you guarantee the outcome of a notice or appeal?
A7: No professional can guarantee an outcome, which depends on the facts, the law and the authority's decision. What we commit to is a thorough, well-documented and timely response.

Q8: What is the due date for filing an income tax return?
A8: For individuals and others whose accounts do not need an audit, the due date is generally 31 July after the end of the year. Later dates apply to cases that require an audit, and the government sometimes extends dates. We track the applicable date for each client.

Q9: Which law applies to my return now?
A9: Income earned up to 31 March 2026 is governed by the Income-tax Act, 1961. From 1 April 2026 the Income-tax Act, 2025 applies, and the "tax year" replaces the earlier "previous year" and "assessment year".

Q10: What are the due dates for GST returns?
A10: For monthly filers, GSTR-1 is due by the 11th and GSTR-3B by the 20th of the following month. Quarterly filers under QRMP in Tamil Nadu file GSTR-3B by the 22nd of the month after the quarter. Dates can be extended by notification.

Q11: What happens if I miss a GST return?
A11: Late fees and interest apply. If returns are pending for a continuous period, e-way bill generation can be blocked and the department can start cancellation of registration.

Q12: How long do I have to reply to an income tax notice?
A12: The reply date is stated in the notice and is often short. If more time is needed, an adjournment can be requested through the portal with reasons. Do not let the date pass without a reply or a request.

Q13: What is the time limit to file an appeal before the Commissioner (Appeals)?
A13: Generally 30 days from receipt of the order. The Tribunal appeal is generally due within 60 days of the appellate order. Delay can be condoned in some cases with sufficient cause.

Q14: What is the time limit for a GST first appeal?
A14: An appeal to the Appellate Authority must be filed within three months of the date of communication of the order. The authority can condone a further delay of up to one month for sufficient cause.

Q15: Is a pre-deposit required to file a GST appeal?
A15: Yes. The admitted amount must be paid in full, along with a pre-deposit of 10% of the disputed tax, subject to a statutory ceiling. A further pre-deposit applies for appeals to the Tribunal.

Q16: What happens if ROC annual returns are filed late?
A16: An additional fee of ₹100 per day of delay applies to each form, with no upper limit. Continued non-filing can lead to penalties on the company and its officers.

Q17: Can a director be disqualified for non-filing?
A17: Yes. If a company fails to file its financial statements or annual returns for three continuous financial years, its directors can be disqualified from being appointed in other companies for five years.

Q18: When is PF registration mandatory?
A18: Establishments with 20 or more employees in covered classes must register under the EPF Act. Smaller establishments can register voluntarily.

Q19: When does ESI apply?
A19: ESI generally applies to establishments with 10 or more employees in notified areas, for employees earning up to ₹21,000 a month.

Q20: Which subsidy schemes am I eligible for?
A20: It depends on your age, education, category, location, sector and project cost. Many schemes are credit-linked and require a bank loan. We assess your profile against current guidelines before recommending a scheme.

Q21: Do I need a bank loan to get a subsidy?
A21: Most MSME subsidy schemes are linked to a bank loan, and the subsidy is released through the bank. Some state incentives are claimed after the unit starts production.

Q22: What is the difference between a DPR and CMA data?
A22: A DPR explains the entire project (technical, market and financial) and is used for new projects and subsidies. CMA data presents past and projected financials in the format banks use to assess working capital and term loan limits.

Q23: How long does it take to prepare a project report?
A23: Usually a few working days after we receive complete information. Larger or more technical projects take longer.

Q24: Is Udyam registration free?
A24: Yes. Registration on the Udyam portal carries no government fee. Our fee covers classification, filing and linking it correctly with your GST and PAN.

Q25: What is the 45-day payment rule?
A25: Under the Income-tax Act, payments due to registered micro and small suppliers that are made beyond the time allowed under the MSME Act are not allowed as a deduction until actually paid. Buyers therefore have a strong reason to pay MSMEs on time.

Q26: What is the due date for depositing TDS?
A26: Tax deducted in a month is generally due by the 7th of the following month. Tax deducted in March has a later due date.

Q27: What happens if a TDS return is filed late?
A27: A late fee of ₹200 per day applies, up to the amount of tax deducted, and penalties can apply for longer delays or incorrect returns.

Q28: Private limited company or LLP: which is better?
A28: A private limited company suits businesses planning to raise equity and scale. An LLP offers limited liability with lighter compliance and suits professional and closely held businesses.

Q29: How long does company registration take?
A29: Typically 7 to 15 working days, depending on how quickly documents are ready and on MCA processing.

Q30: Can you work on our existing Tally data?
A30: Yes. We can work on your existing software and data, remotely or at your office, depending on the arrangement.

Q31: Trust, society or Section 8 company: which should we choose?
A31: It depends on the number of founders, how you want the institution governed, and the activities planned. We explain the differences before you decide.
