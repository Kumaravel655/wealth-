[ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS & ANSWERS ]

Q1: What is the time limit for a GST first appeal?
A1: An appeal to the Appellate Authority must be filed within three months of the date of communication of the order. The authority can condone a further delay of up to one month for sufficient cause.

Q2: Is a pre-deposit required to file a GST appeal?
A2: Yes. The admitted amount must be paid in full, along with a pre-deposit of 10% of the disputed tax, subject to a statutory ceiling. A further pre-deposit applies for appeals to the Tribunal.

Q3: I agree with the discrepancy in an ASMT-10 notice. What should I do?
A3: You can pay the tax with interest through Form DRC-03 and inform the officer in your reply. This usually closes the scrutiny without further proceedings.

Q4: Can the penalty in a show cause notice be reduced?
A4: In non-fraud cases, paying the tax and interest within the time allowed after the notice can significantly reduce or remove the penalty. The options depend on the section and period involved.

Q5: Can you help if the GST department issues summons?
A5: Yes. We help you understand the scope of the enquiry, prepare the documents and statements required, and coordinate with counsel where the matter needs it.
