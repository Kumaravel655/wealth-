[ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS & ANSWERS ]

Q1: What are the due dates for GST returns?
A1: For monthly filers, GSTR-1 is due by the 11th and GSTR-3B by the 20th of the following month. Quarterly filers under QRMP in Tamil Nadu file GSTR-3B by the 22nd of the month after the quarter. Dates can be extended by notification.

Q2: What happens if I miss a GST return?
A2: Late fees and interest apply. If returns are pending for a continuous period, e-way bill generation can be blocked and the department can start cancellation of registration.

Q3: What is the QRMP scheme?
A3: Taxpayers with aggregate turnover up to ₹5 crore can file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B quarterly while paying tax monthly. It reduces the number of returns but not the payment discipline.

Q4: Do I need to file GSTR-9?
A4: GSTR-9 is the annual return. Small taxpayers below the notified turnover limit have been exempted in recent years, and GSTR-9C applies to larger taxpayers. We confirm your requirement each year.

Q5: Why is my input tax credit lower than my purchase invoices?
A5: Credit is available only when the supplier reports the invoice in their GSTR-1 and it appears in your GSTR-2B, subject to other conditions. Our monthly reconciliation identifies such gaps early so you can follow up with suppliers.
