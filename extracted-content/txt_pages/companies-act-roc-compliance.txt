[ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS & ANSWERS ]

Q1: What happens if ROC annual returns are filed late?
A1: An additional fee of ₹100 per day of delay applies to each form, with no upper limit. Continued non-filing can lead to penalties on the company and its officers.

Q2: Can a director be disqualified for non-filing?
A2: Yes. If a company fails to file its financial statements or annual returns for three continuous financial years, its directors can be disqualified from being appointed in other companies for five years.

Q3: What is adjudication under the Companies Act?
A3: For many defaults, the ROC acts as an adjudicating officer and imposes penalties after issuing a notice and giving an opportunity of being heard. A well-prepared reply can make a real difference to the outcome.

Q4: Can a struck-off company be revived?
A4: Yes, within the time allowed by law, through an application for restoration. We prepare the filings and documents needed for the process.
