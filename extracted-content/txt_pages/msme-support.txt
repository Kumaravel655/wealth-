[ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS & ANSWERS ]

Q1: Is Udyam registration free?
A1: Yes. Registration on the Udyam portal carries no government fee. Our fee covers classification, filing and linking it correctly with your GST and PAN.

Q2: What is the 45-day payment rule?
A2: Under the Income-tax Act, payments due to registered micro and small suppliers that are made beyond the time allowed under the MSME Act are not allowed as a deduction until actually paid. Buyers therefore have a strong reason to pay MSMEs on time.

Q3: What can I do if a buyer delays payment?
A3: A registered micro or small enterprise can file a delayed payment application through the MSME Samadhaan portal for the Facilitation Council's consideration. We prepare the application and supporting documents.

Q4: Can MSMEs get loans without collateral?
A4: Eligible loans to micro and small enterprises can be covered under the CGTMSE credit guarantee, which allows banks to lend without collateral security.
