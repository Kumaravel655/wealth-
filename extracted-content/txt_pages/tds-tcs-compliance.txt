[ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS & ANSWERS ]

Q1: What is the due date for depositing TDS?
A1: Tax deducted in a month is generally due by the 7th of the following month. Tax deducted in March has a later due date.

Q2: What happens if a TDS return is filed late?
A2: A late fee of ₹200 per day applies, up to the amount of tax deducted, and penalties can apply for longer delays or incorrect returns.
