[ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS & ANSWERS ]

Q1: Private limited company or LLP: which is better?
A1: A private limited company suits businesses planning to raise equity and scale. An LLP offers limited liability with lighter compliance and suits professional and closely held businesses.

Q2: How long does company registration take?
A2: Typically 7 to 15 working days, depending on how quickly documents are ready and on MCA processing.
