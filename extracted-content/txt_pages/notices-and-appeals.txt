[ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS & ANSWERS ]

Q1: I received a notice. What should I do first?
A1: Note the reply date, and do not ignore the notice or let the date pass. Send us the notice with your returns for the period. We will tell you what is being asked, what documents are needed and how much time you have.

Q2: Can you handle notices from more than one department?
A2: Yes. We handle income tax, GST, Companies Act (ROC) and PF and ESI proceedings, which helps when one issue affects several laws, such as a turnover difference between GST and income tax returns.

Q3: What if the order goes against me?
A3: We review the order, advise on rectification where there is an apparent mistake, and prepare an appeal within the time limit where the order is wrong in law or on facts.
